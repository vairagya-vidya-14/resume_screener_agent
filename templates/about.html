Our Story

Brewing Happiness Since 2015

Viyora Café was born out of a passion for coffee and community. Our journey started with a small dream to create a space where people can relax, connect and enjoy exceptional coffee handcrafted from ethically sourced beans.

Every cup we serve is a tribute to master craftsmanship, warm hospitality, and the fine art of coffee roasting. Whether you are grabbing a quick morning espresso or spending an afternoon reading, Viyora Café is your home away from home.
